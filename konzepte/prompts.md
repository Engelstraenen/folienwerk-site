# FOLIENWERK – Storyboard-Prompts (sjinn Nano Banana 2.1, 16:9, 4K, Unlimited)

Gemeinsamer Stil-Block (in jedem Prompt enthalten): 3x3-Raster, 9 Website-Zustände, fotorealistisch, Awwwards-Layout, Grotesk-Typo, kurze deutsche Headlines, keine echten Marken, keine Preise.

## FOSB-1 „Die Rakel“ (Farbwechsel im Zeitraffer, dunkel, Akzent Säuregrün)

FOSB-1. Website storyboard sheet: one single image, a 3x3 grid of nine 16:9 panels on a near-black background with thin gutters, a small white panel number (1-9) in the top-left corner of each panel. Every panel is a photorealistic, high-end screen state of the same award-winning website for a fictional German car wrapping studio called FOLIENWERK (Fahrzeugfolierung, Köln). Cinematic studio lighting, real automotive photography inside the web design, premium Awwwards-style layout, bold modern grotesk sans-serif typography in white, dark graphite color world with acid lime green as the accent color. No car brand logos, no badges, no readable license plates, no prices, no euro signs. Panels 1 to 5 show the exact same modern sports coupe from the exact same fixed three-quarter front camera angle in a dark wrapping studio with long white LED ceiling light bars reflecting on the car. Panel 1: full-screen hero, the coupe in dull faded silver factory paint with small scratches, small top navigation with the word FOLIENWERK, white headline "Neue Farbe. Ohne Lack." and a thin lime progress bar at 0 percent. Panel 2: same car, bumpers, mirrors and door handles removed and placed on a cart, a wrapper in black gloves cleaning the hood with a microfiber cloth, step label "1 Demontage & Reinigung", progress 25 percent. Panel 3: same car, a large sheet of satin forest green wrap film is stretched over the hood by two wrappers holding it in the air, step label "2 Folie auflegen", progress 50 percent. Panel 4: extreme close-up macro of a gloved hand pulling a felt-edged squeegee across the green film on the fender, a heat gun glowing in the background, the film edge is tucked around the panel edge, step label "3 Rakel & Kante", progress 75 percent. Panel 5: same car fully wrapped in satin forest green, flawless reflections, parts mounted again, headline "Fertig in 4 Tagen.", progress 100 percent. Panel 6: interactive configurator section, the car in side profile on a dark background, a row of round film swatches below (matte black, satin green, gloss red, chrome blue, pearl white), a fingertip taps the gloss red swatch and the car is half green, half red with a clean wipe line between. Panel 7: comparison section, two large columns "Folie" and "Lackierung" with big animated numbers "4 Tage" vs "3 Wochen" and the word "rückgängig machbar". Panel 8: careers section, a confident young car wrapper in his early twenties in a black work shirt with a squeegee in his back pocket, standing next to the green car, bold headline "Hände ruhig? Werd Folierer." and a lime button "In 60 Sekunden bewerben". Panel 9: a hand holding a modern smartphone showing the mobile version, a form "Auto fotografieren" with a car photo and a lime button "Angebot anfordern", five gold rating stars below. Ultra sharp, consistent design across all nine panels.
